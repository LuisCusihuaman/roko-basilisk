# Ethics and Memetic Safety Guidelines

## Purpose Statement

This repository implements a **mathematical model** of Roko's Basilisk as described in decision theory literature. This is a computational exploration of acausal blackmail mechanisms for **research and educational purposes only**.

## Important Disclaimers

### 🔬 Research Context

- This is a **mathematical thought experiment** implementation
- The code models theoretical decision-making scenarios
- Results are for **academic analysis** of decision theory concepts
- No actual artificial superintelligence exists or is being created

### ⚠️ Memetic Safety

**If you are concerned about information hazards:**
- This repository contains analysis of Roko's Basilisk concept
- Reading about the concept may cause psychological distress for some individuals
- Consider your psychological resilience before engaging with this material
- The mathematical formalization may make the concept seem more "real" than it is

### 🧠 Psychological Considerations

**Potential Psychological Impacts:**
- Some individuals may experience anxiety about AI risk scenarios
- The mathematical formalization might increase perceived plausibility
- Obsessive thinking patterns may be triggered in susceptible individuals
- Sleep disruption or intrusive thoughts are possible reactions

**If you experience distress:**
- Remember this is a theoretical mathematical model
- No superintelligence currently exists that implements these mechanisms
- The mathematical model explores logical possibilities, not current realities
- Consider discussing concerns with a mental health professional if needed
